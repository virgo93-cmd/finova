# Finova Play Store Listing Draft

## App name

Finova: Money & Habit Tracker

## Short description

Track money, budgets, tasks, and habits privately in one calm local-first app.

## Full description

Take control of your money and your day with Finova.

Finova combines a fast personal expense tracker with practical productivity tools. Record income and expenses in seconds, understand your monthly cash flow, create realistic budgets, organize tasks, and build habits—all in one clean experience.

### Money made clear

- Record income and expenses quickly
- Organize transactions with useful or custom categories
- Track your current balance and monthly cash flow
- Set overall and category budgets
- Review spending patterns and category insights

### Build steady routines

- Plan daily and upcoming tasks
- Set task priorities and track completion
- Create daily or selected-day habits
- View current and longest streaks based on real check-ins
- Review productivity progress over time

### Private by design

Core financial and productivity records are stored locally on your device. No account is required for core tracking.

Finova is designed to be simple first and powerful when needed.

Track Money. Track Life.
