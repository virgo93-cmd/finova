# Owner Inputs Required Before Release

Only the publisher can supply or approve these external/account-specific items:

1. Legal publisher/developer name
2. Public support email
3. Publisher country/address and governing jurisdiction
4. Confirm the finalized Android application ID (`com.finova.app`; changing it later creates a different Play app identity)
5. Public website or hosting location for Privacy Policy and Terms
6. Google Play Console developer account access
7. No further AdMob IDs required; App, banner, interstitial, and rewarded IDs are configured
8. Play App Signing enrollment or securely stored upload key and passwords
9. Intended audience/age declaration, content-rating questionnaire answers, and Data Safety approval
10. Final approval of store copy, screenshots, pricing, countries, and release track

Never send signing-key passwords or private keys through chat. Store them in a secure password manager or CI secret store.
