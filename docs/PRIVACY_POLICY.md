# Finova Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 2, 2026**

Finova ("Finova", "we", "our", or "the app") is a personal finance and productivity application. This policy explains what information the app handles and the choices available to you. The publisher must replace the bracketed contact details before public release.

## Information you enter

Finova lets you record transactions, categories, budgets, tasks, habits, habit check-ins, preferences, and an optional starting balance. Core records are stored locally on your device. Finova does not require an account and the current version does not transmit these records to a Finova-operated server.

## Device storage and deletion

Core data remains in the app's private local storage until you delete individual records, use **Reset all data**, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling or clearing storage may permanently remove your records. Keep any records you need outside the app before resetting it.

## Advertising and consent

Finova uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements. Google and its partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, approximate location inferred from network information, ad interactions, diagnostics, and consent choices. Depending on your region and settings, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. Finova uses Google's consent mechanism where required. You can learn more in [Google's advertising privacy information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## Notifications

If you enable reminders, Finova may request notification permission and schedule local notifications on your device. Reminder content and scheduling are handled locally. You can disable notifications in Finova or your device settings.

## Permissions

Finova may request internet access for advertising and notification permission for reminders. The core tracking features work without an account. Permissions can be managed through your operating-system settings.

## Children

Finova is not directed to children under the minimum age required to consent to data processing in their country. The publisher must configure the app's store audience and advertising settings consistently with the intended audience.

## Security

We use platform-provided application storage and reasonable technical safeguards. No storage method is completely secure. Protect access to your device and install operating-system security updates.

## International processing

Advertising providers may process information in countries other than your own, subject to their policies and applicable safeguards.

## Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.
